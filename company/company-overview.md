# Nexora Technologies — Company Overview

Nexora Technologies is a fictional B2B SaaS company that provides commerce, billing, and customer-support software to mid-market and enterprise customers.

Nexora operates three primary products:
- **CommerceCloud** — order, catalog, checkout, and customer-facing commerce capabilities.
- **BillingPro** — subscription billing, invoices, payment orchestration, and revenue reporting.
- **SupportHub** — customer support workflows, ticketing, notifications, and agent tooling.

The platform is organized around shared services including API Gateway, Identity Service, Payment Service, Order Service, Notification Service, Search Service, Customer Profile Service, and Data Platform.

The company uses a service-oriented architecture. Teams own services, projects modify one or more services, and incidents are tracked against affected services and customers.

Key teams referenced throughout this knowledge base are Payments Engineering, Identity Platform, Core Platform, Customer Experience, Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), and Security Engineering.
