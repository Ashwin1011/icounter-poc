# CUST-NORTHSTAR — Northstar Retail

Northstar Retail is an enterprise customer using CommerceCloud for online checkout and order management.

### Products
CommerceCloud

### Important services
API Gateway, Identity Service, Order Service, Payment Service, Customer Profile Service, Notification Service.

### Operational notes
Northstar is sensitive to checkout latency. Payment authorization incidents are treated as high business impact for this customer.

### Related incidents
INC-1001 affected Northstar Retail during elevated payment authorization latency.
