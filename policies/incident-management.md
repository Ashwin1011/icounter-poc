# Policy — Incident Management

Nexora requires documented incident ownership, severity classification, timelines, and follow-up actions.

### Requirements
- Every Sev-1 and Sev-2 incident must have an incident commander.
- The service-owning team is accountable for technical mitigation.
- SRE coordinates incident command for Sev-1 unless another owner is explicitly assigned.
- Customer impact must be recorded where known.
- Root cause and follow-up actions should be documented after recovery.
