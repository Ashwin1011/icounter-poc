# TEAM-PAY — Payments Engineering

Payments Engineering owns the Payment Service (SVC-PAY) and the payment-related portions of BillingPro (PROD-BILL) and CommerceCloud (PROD-COM).

**Manager:** Elena Rao
**Technical Lead:** Marcus Chen
**Primary on-call rotation:** PAY-PRIMARY

The team owns payment authorization, capture, refunds, payment-method tokenization, and connections to external payment processors. The team does not own customer authentication; that responsibility belongs to Identity Platform.

Payments Engineering collaborates closely with SRE for production incidents and with Security Engineering for payment-data controls.

Recent projects involving the team include Project Apollo (PROJ-APOLLO) and Project Mercury (PROJ-MERCURY).
