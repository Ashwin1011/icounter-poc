# TEAM-SRE — Site Reliability Engineering

SRE provides the company-wide reliability function. The team does not own most application services but operates shared observability, incident management, deployment guardrails, and production access workflows.

**Manager:** Hannah Brooks
**Technical Lead:** Sameer Kulkarni
**Primary on-call rotation:** SRE-PRIMARY

SRE is the incident commander for Sev-1 incidents unless an exception is documented. SRE works with service-owning teams on incidents, capacity planning, and reliability reviews.

Project Mercury includes a cross-team initiative to reduce payment authorization latency.
